# Code of conduct

This is a scientific project. We disagree about results often; we do it about the
results, with evidence, and never about the person.

- Be courteous and patient, especially with newcomers and students.
- Credit others' work precisely. Do not present anyone's work as your own.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, in any project space.
- When you point out an error in someone's paper, say what you checked and how, and
  assume good faith: misprints happen to everyone.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainers (see GOVERNANCE.md). They will
be handled confidentially. Persistent or serious breaches lead to removal from the
project's spaces.

This code follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org), in our own words.
