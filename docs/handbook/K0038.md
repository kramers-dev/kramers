# K0038. Diffusion with resetting and a refractory period: mean time to the target

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

If after each reset the particle must wait a random refractory period before it moves again, the mean time to reach the target depends only on the mean of that period (given the time since the previous reset). With Poissonian resetting at rate r and a constant mean period, it is (1 + r a0) times the mean without the period.

## Model

$$ \text{free diffusion from } X_r=L\ \text{to an absorbing target; resets at rate } r\text{, each followed by a frozen period } \tau,\ \langle\tau|t\rangle=a_0+a_1t $$

## Result

$$ T=\frac{\tilde Q_0(r)+r\left[a_0\tilde Q_0(r)-a_1\tilde Q_0'(r)\right]}{1-r\tilde Q_0(r)},\quad \tilde Q_0(s)=\frac{1-e^{-L\sqrt{s/D}}}{s} $$

```text
Mean time for a diffusing particle to reach a target at distance L, resetting at rate r, with a refractory
period after each reset whose mean, given the time t since the previous reset, is a0 + a1 t.

Evans and Majumdar's Eq. (41) with the free-diffusion survival:
    T = [Q~0(r) + r (a0 Q~0(r) - a1 Q~0'(r))] / (1 - r Q~0(r)),  Q~0(s) = (1 - e^{-L sqrt(s/D)})/s.
The particle is frozen at the reset point during a refractory period; only the conditional mean of
the period enters. a0 = a1 = 0 is entry K0015; for a1 = 0 the mean is (1 + r a0)(e^{L sqrt(r/D)} - 1)/r
(derived here, not printed). <x^2> = 2 D t.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `L` | distance from the reset point to the target |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient (<x^2> = 2Dt) |
| `r` | resetting rate |
| `a0` | mean refractory period (constant part) |
| `a1` | growth of the mean period with the time since the previous reset |

## When it holds

Free diffusion in one dimension between resets, target absorbing, Poissonian resetting, each reset followed by a refractory period during which the particle stays at the reset point; the process does not start with a period. Exact. Only the conditional mean of the period enters.
