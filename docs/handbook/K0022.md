# K0022. Effective diffusion in a tilted periodic potential

**Area:** Transport in periodic potentials. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

The long-time effective diffusion coefficient of an overdamped particle in a tilted periodic potential, as one integral over a period. Near the critical tilt it can exceed free diffusion by orders of magnitude (giant acceleration of diffusion).

## Model

$$ \eta\dot x=-V'(x)+F+\sqrt{2\eta k_BT}\,\xi(t),\quad V(x+L)=V(x) $$

## Result

$$ D=D_0\frac{\int_{x_0}^{x_0+L}\frac{dx}{L}I_+^2(x)I_-(x)}{\left[\int_{x_0}^{x_0+L}\frac{dx}{L}I_+(x)\right]^3} $$

## When it holds

One-dimensional overdamped motion, white noise, any periodic V and tilt. Exact.
