# K0001. Kramers' escape rate, overdamped (Smoluchowski) limit

**Area:** Escape over a barrier. **Kind:** asymptotic (leading term of an expansion; its error is mapped against an exact reference).

For strong friction and a barrier high compared with kT, the rate of escape from a potential well is the geometric mean of the curvatures at the well bottom and the barrier top, divided by 2 pi times the friction coefficient, times the Arrhenius factor. It is too high by corrections of relative order kT/barrier: 3 to 6% at 10 kT for the cubic, quartic double-well and cosine shapes mapped here.

## Model

$$ \zeta\dot x = -U'(x) + \sqrt{2\zeta k_BT}\,\xi(t),\quad \Delta U = U(x_b)-U(x_a) \gg k_BT $$

## Result

$$ k = \frac{\sqrt{U''(x_a)\,|U''(x_b)|}}{2\pi\zeta}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta U}{k_BT}\right) $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import kramers_rate_overdamped
kramers_rate_overdamped(curvature_well: 'float', curvature_barrier: 'float', barrier: 'float', friction: 'float', kT: 'float' = 1.0) -> 'float'
```

```text
k = sqrt(U''(a) |U''(b)|) / (2 pi friction) * exp(-barrier / kT).

Parameters
----------
curvature_well
    U''(a) > 0 at the bottom of the well (energy / length^2).
curvature_barrier
    |U''(b)| at the top of the barrier.
barrier
    U(b) - U(a) > 0.
friction
    Friction coefficient zeta in zeta dx/dt = -U'(x) + noise (so D = kT / zeta).
    It is M gamma in the notation of Haenggi, Talkner and Borkovec, whose gamma is
    a damping rate.
kT
    Thermal energy.

Warns ``ValidityWarning`` when the barrier is below 10 kT, where the formula is
several per cent high (see ``VALIDITY_MAP``).
```

| `curvature_barrier` | |U''(x_b)| |

| `friction` | friction coefficient zeta (D = kT/zeta) |

## Where it is printed

- **Primary source:** P. Haenggi, P. Talkner and M. Borkovec, Rev. Mod. Phys. 62, 251 (1990). [doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.62.251](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.62.251)
  - Where: Eq. (4.34), p. 272; also (4.54), p. 274, and (7.10), p. 291.
  - Read: scan of the journal pages, Augsburg university repository (opus4 file 41911); p. 291 viewed 14 Oct 2026.
  - As printed: $ k^{\rm overdamped}_{A\to C}=\frac{\omega_0\,\omega_b}{2\pi\gamma}\exp(-\beta E_b) $
  - Notation: M omega_0^2 = U''(x_a), M omega_b^2 = |U''(x_b)|, E_b = Delta U, beta = 1/kT; their gamma is a damping rate (Eq. 4.1), so M gamma = zeta, our friction coefficient.
- **Original work:** H. A. Kramers, Physica 7, 284 (1940). [doi:10.1016/S0031-8914(40)90098-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0031-8914(40)90098-2)
  - Where: the original derivation.
  - Read: not opened (closed access); citation only.

## When it holds

Overdamped motion (friction large compared with the barrier frequency), barrier large compared with kT, smooth parabolic well and barrier. Overestimate against the exact rate (computed check): cubic well 7.0%, 3.2%, 1.5% at 5, 10, 20 kT; quartic double well 10.0%, 4.3%, 2.0%; cosine well 13.4%, 5.8%, 2.7%.
