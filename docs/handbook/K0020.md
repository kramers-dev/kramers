# K0020. Sparre Andersen theorem: universal survival probability of a symmetric random walk

**Area:** Active and anomalous motion. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

For a random walk on the line with independent jumps drawn from any symmetric, continuous distribution (Levy flights included), the probability of staying positive for n steps is binom(2n, n)/4^n, which decays as 1/sqrt(pi n).

## Model

$$ x_n = x_{n-1}+\xi_n,\ x_0=0,\ \xi_n\ \text{i.i.d., symmetric, continuous} $$

## Result

$$ q(n)={2n\choose n}2^{-2n},\quad \sum_{n\ge0}q(n)s^n=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-s}},\quad q(n)\sim\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi n}} $$

## When it holds

Symmetric and continuous jump density; start at the origin. Exact for every n. Lattice walks differ.
