# K0030. Switching of the noisy fold under a linear ramp, solved exactly (computed reference)

**Area:** Driven escape and switching under a ramp. **Kind:** computed-reference (computed here to stated precision; no closed form is claimed).

When the control parameter of a noisy saddle-node is ramped towards the fold at any speed, the distribution of the distance at which paths escape, computed from the time-dependent Fokker-Planck equation with no quasi-static assumption and no fitted coefficient. At fast ramps the quasi-static (Kramers or exact-rate) answer is off by tens of per cent and many paths escape only after the fold.

## Model

$$ dy=\left(y^2-u_0+\Sigma s\right)ds+\sqrt2\,dW,\ \text{escape at } y=y_c $$

## Result

$$ S(s)=\int p(y,s)\,dy,\quad \partial_s p=-\partial_y[(y^2-u(s))p]+\partial_y^2p,\ p(y_c,s)=0 $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import ramped_fold_switching
ramped_fold_switching(sigma: 'float', levels: 'tuple[float, ...]' = (0.1, 0.5, 0.9), u0: 'float' = 7.0, y_escape: 'float' = 9.0, n_cells: 'int' = 1500) -> 'np.ndarray'
```

```text
Distance to the fold, u, at which the fractions ``levels`` of paths have escaped.

Model (scaled): dy = (y^2 - u(s)) ds + sqrt(2) dW with u(s) = u0 - sigma s; paths start
in the quasi-stationary well (a Gaussian around -sqrt(u0)) and escape when y reaches
``y_escape``. Negative values are past the fold. Computed by the time-dependent
Fokker-Planck solver; no fitted coefficient and no quasi-static assumption.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sigma` | scaled ramp rate |
| `levels` | escaped fractions |
| `u0` | starting distance |
| `y_escape` | escape point |
| `n_cells` | grid size |

## Where it is printed

This entry is a computed reference: no closed form is claimed. Its checks below are its evidence.


## When it holds

Scaled ramp rates from about 1e-3 to 1 and beyond; the solver's own error is below 1e-3 in u.

## How it is checked

| Check | Against | Latest result |
|---|---|---|
| quantiles against direct simulation (slow, nightly) | Euler-Maruyama, 6,000 paths, the same escape criterion | pass: worst 1.7e+00 (tolerance 4e+00), 2026-10-14 |
| slow ramp: approaches the quasi-static median (slow, nightly) | decay rate of the frozen fold (K0008) | pass: worst 2.3e-03 (tolerance 1e-02), 2026-10-14 |

## Notes

Replaces the lag coefficient fitted to simulations in geoews 0.3 (dev branch). A prototype of 12 October 2026 matched the author's simulated medians (0.748, 0.611, 0.487, 0.338 at Sigma = 0.08, 0.10, 0.122, 0.15; statistical errors 0.003 to 0.005) to within 0.005 after converting between the two escape criteria.
