# K0036. Resetting between an absorbing end and a reflecting wall: mean time and the critical start point

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A Brownian particle between an absorbing end at 0 and a reflecting wall at L, reset to its start x0 at rate r, is absorbed after a mean time [cosh(aL)/cosh(a(L - x0)) - 1]/r with a = sqrt(r/D). Resetting shortens the mean only when the particle starts closer to the target than x0/L = 1 - 1/sqrt(5) = 0.553.

## Model

$$ \partial_t p=D\,\partial_x^2p-r\,p+r\,\delta(x-x_0)\,Q(t),\ x\in(0,L),\ p(0,t)=0,\ \partial_xp(L,t)=0 $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_r\rangle=\frac{1}{r}\left[\frac{\cosh(\sqrt{r/D}\,L)}{\cosh(\sqrt{r/D}\,(L-x_0))}-1\right],\quad \frac{x_0}{L}<1-\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\ \text{for resetting to help} $$

```text
Mean time to reach an absorbing end at 0 from x0, with a reflecting wall at L and resetting to x0 at rate r.

<T_r> = [cosh(a L) / cosh(a (L - x0)) - 1] / r with a = sqrt(r/D) (Eq. 26). Evaluated through
cosh u - cosh v = 2 sinh((u+v)/2) sinh((u-v)/2), in logarithms, so there is no cancellation as
r -> 0 and no overflow for large r. <x^2> = 2 D t.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `x0` | start and reset position, 0 < x0 < L |
| `L` | position of the reflecting wall (target at 0) |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient (<x^2> = 2Dt) |
| `r` | resetting rate |

## When it holds

One dimension, free diffusion between an absorbing end and a reflecting wall, Poissonian instantaneous resetting to the start. Exact. Not the two-absorbing-end interval of K0032.
