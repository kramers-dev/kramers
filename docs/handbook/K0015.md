# K0015. Diffusion with stochastic resetting: mean time to reach the target

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A Brownian particle that restarts from x0 at random times (rate r) reaches a target at the origin after a finite mean time, (exp(sqrt(r/D) x0) - 1)/r, although without resetting the mean is infinite.

## Model

$$ \partial_t p = D\,\partial_x^2 p - r\,p + r\,\delta(x-x_0),\ \text{absorbing at } 0 $$

## Result

$$ T(x_0)=\frac{1}{r}\left(e^{\alpha_0x_0}-1\right),\quad \alpha_0=\sqrt{r/D} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import resetting_mean_time
resetting_mean_time(x0: 'float', r: 'float', D: 'float') -> 'float'
```

```text
Mean first-passage time to the origin from x0 > 0, resetting to x0 at rate r: (exp(sqrt(r/D) x0) - 1) / r.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `x0` | start and reset position |
| `r` | resetting rate |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient |

## When it holds

One dimension; Poissonian, instantaneous resetting to the starting point. Exact.
