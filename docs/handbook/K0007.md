# K0007. Rate near a saddle-node to first order in the bias

**Area:** Neuron models. **Kind:** asymptotic (leading term of an expansion; its error is mapped against an exact reference).

For weak bias (or strong noise), the rate of passage for dx/dt = beta + x^2 + sqrt(2D) xi is 0.201 D^(1/3) + 0.147 D^(-1/3) beta, with both coefficients known in closed form.

## Model

$$ \dot x = \beta + x^2 + \sqrt{2D}\,\xi(t),\ |\beta| \ll D^{2/3} $$

## Result

$$ r \approx \frac{(3D)^{1/3}}{[\Gamma(1/3)]^2} + \frac{9}{8}\frac{3^{1/6}}{\pi^3}[\Gamma(2/3)]^4 D^{-1/3}\beta \approx 0.201\,D^{1/3} + 0.147\,D^{-1/3}\beta $$
