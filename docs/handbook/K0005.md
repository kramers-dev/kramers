# K0005. Noisy saddle-node exactly at the bifurcation: mean, variance and CV of the passage time

**Area:** Escape over a barrier. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

At the saddle-node bifurcation point, the time to pass from minus to plus infinity under dx/dt = x^2 + sqrt(2D) xi has mean Gamma(1/3)^2 (3D)^(-1/3) and variance one third of the mean squared, so its coefficient of variation is 1/sqrt(3) whatever the noise intensity.

## Model

$$ \dot x = x^2 + \sqrt{2D}\,\xi(t),\quad x(0)=-\infty,\ \text{passage to } +\infty $$

## Result

$$ \langle T\rangle=\Gamma(\tfrac13)^2(3D)^{-1/3},\quad \langle\Delta T^2\rangle=\tfrac13\langle T\rangle^2,\quad CV=1/\sqrt3 $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import saddle_node_threshold_statistics

```

```text
Statistics of the passage from -inf to +inf of dx/dt = x^2 + sqrt(2D) xi(t).

This is the type I (quadratic integrate-and-fire, theta) neuron biased exactly at
rheobase: mean interspike interval, its variance, the firing rate and the
coefficient of variation.
```

## When it holds

White Gaussian noise, thresholds at plus and minus infinity, bias exactly zero. Exact.
