# K0018. Restart helps at small rates exactly when the coefficient of variation exceeds one

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

Introducing restart at a small rate r changes the mean completion time by r times (<T>^2 - var T)/2: it lowers the mean if and only if the standard deviation of the completion time exceeds its mean.

## Model

$$ \text{as K0017} $$

## Result

$$ \left.\frac{d\langle T_r\rangle}{dr}\right|_{r=0}=\frac{\langle T\rangle^2-\sigma^2(T)}{2},\quad \text{restart helps}\iff \frac{\sigma(T)}{\langle T\rangle}>1 $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import restart_initial_slope
restart_initial_slope(mean: 'float', second_moment: 'float') -> 'float'
```

```text
d<T_r>/dr at r = 0: <T>^2 - <T^2>/2 = (<T>^2 - var T)/2. Negative (restart helps) iff CV > 1.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `mean` | <T> without restart |
| `second_moment` | <T^2> without restart |

## When it holds

Finite second moment. Local in r: CV < 1 does not exclude a gain at an intermediate rate.
