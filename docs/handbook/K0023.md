# K0023. Mean exit time of a run-and-tumble particle from an interval

**Area:** Active and anomalous motion. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A run-and-tumble particle (speed v, tumble rate alpha) starting at a reflecting wall and moving away from it reaches an absorbing point at distance L after a mean time L/v + L^2/(2 D0), the ballistic time plus a diffusive time with D0 = v^2/alpha.

## Model

$$ \partial_tP_R=-\partial_x(vP_R)-\tfrac{\alpha}{2}P_R+\tfrac{\alpha}{2}P_L,\ \text{mirror for } P_L;\ J(0)=0,\ P_L(L)=0 $$

## Result

$$ \tau=\frac{L}{v}+\frac{L^2}{2D_0},\quad D_0=\frac{v^2}{\alpha} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import run_and_tumble_exit_time
run_and_tumble_exit_time(L: 'float', v: 'float', alpha: 'float') -> 'float'
```

```text
Mean time for a run-and-tumble particle started at 0 moving right to reach L.

Reflecting wall at 0, absorbing at L, speed v, tumble rate alpha (new direction drawn
at random, so the direction reverses at rate alpha / 2), no translational diffusion:
L / v + L^2 / (2 D0) with D0 = v^2 / alpha (Angelani et al. Eq. 11).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `L` | distance to the absorbing point |
| `v` | swimming speed |
| `alpha` | tumble rate |

## When it holds

One dimension, constant speed and tumble rate, no translational diffusion, start at the wall moving inwards. Exact.
