# K0037. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process with resetting, target at the trap centre: mean time and the vanishing optimal rate

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A particle in a harmonic trap, dx = -kappa x dt + sqrt(2D) dW, reset to x0 at rate r, reaches the centre of the trap after a mean time given in closed form by a parabolic cylinder function. Resetting shortens the mean only when the start is close enough to the centre: x0 sqrt(kappa/D) < 1.0455, where the spread of the time without resetting equals its mean.

## Model

$$ dx=-\kappa x\,dt+\sqrt{2D}\,dW,\ \text{absorbing at } x=0,\ \text{reset to } x_0 \text{ at rate } r $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_r\rangle=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}\,2^{-\nu/2}e^{-z^2/4}}{r\,\Gamma\!\left(\frac{\nu+1}{2}\right)D_{-\nu}(z)}-\frac1r,\quad \nu=\frac{r}{\kappa},\ z=x_0\sqrt{\kappa/D} $$

```text
Mean time to reach the centre x = 0 of the trap from x0, for dx = -kappa x dt + sqrt(2D) dW,
resetting to x0 at rate r.

Dubey and Pal's Eq. (41), with D restored (z = x0 sqrt(kappa/D), nu = r/kappa):
    <T_r> = sqrt(pi) 2^(-nu/2) e^(-z^2/4) / (r Gamma((nu+1)/2) D_(-nu)(z)) - 1/r,
D_(-nu) the parabolic cylinder function. Evaluated through the integral representation of D_(-nu),
integrated by parts so that nothing diverges as nu -> 0: 1/T~(r) = sqrt(pi) 2^(-nu/2)
Gamma(nu+1) / (Gamma((nu+1)/2) J(nu, z)). Ahmad et al. write the same mean with the potential
k x^2 (so kappa = 2k) in Kummer functions (Eqs. 21-22). Relative accuracy falls as about
1e-16 kappa/r when r is tiny compared with kappa.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `x0` | start and reset position (> 0); the target is the trap centre |
| `kappa` | trap stiffness (drift -kappa x) |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient |
| `r` | resetting rate |

## Where it is printed

- **Primary source:** A. Dubey and A. Pal, J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 56, 435002 (2023). [doi:10.1088/1751-8121/acf748](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/acf748)
  - Where: Eq. (41) p. 12; critical stiffness k* = 0.174895 at x_R = 2.5 (Fig. 4 caption) (arXiv:2304.05226v1 numbering).
  - Read: arXiv:2304.05226v1 TeX source and PDF text layer, opened 22 Nov 2026; journal numbering not checked.
  - As printed: $ \langle t_f(r) \rangle = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}2^{-\frac{r}{2k}}e^{-kx_R^2/4}}{r\Gamma(\frac{r+k}{2k})D_{-\frac{r}{k}}(\sqrt{k}x_R)}-\frac{1}{r} $
  - Notation: their k is kappa and D = 1; for other D replace x_R by x0/sqrt(D) (a rescaling, not printed). The quadrature solver puts the critical stiffness at k* = 0.174898 for x_R = 2.5, against the printed 0.174895.
- **Also printed in:** S. Ahmad, I. Nayak, A. Bansal, A. Nandi and D. Das, Phys. Rev. E 99, 022130 (2019). [doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.99.022130](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.99.022130)
  - Where: Eqs. (21)-(22) p. 4 (mean, Kummer functions); K_c = 0.7393 (p. 4, Fig. 2 caption); Eq. (6) (transition).
  - Read: arXiv:1902.02955v1 TeX source and PDF p. 4, opened 22 Nov 2026 (two readers); journal numbering not checked.
  - As printed: $ \langle T_r \rangle= \frac{1}{r}\bigg[\frac{1}{G(x_{0},s=0)}-1\bigg],\ G =F_{1}\Big(\frac{\alpha^2}{4\gamma};\frac{1}{2};\frac{kx^{2}}{D}\Big)-2\frac{\Gamma(\frac{\alpha^2}{4\gamma}+\frac{1}{2})}{\Gamma(\frac{\alpha^2}{4\gamma})} x \sqrt{\gamma} F_{1}\Big(\frac{\alpha^2}{4\gamma}+\frac{1}{2};\frac{3}{2};\frac{kx^{2}}{D}\Big) $
  - Notation: their potential is k x^2, so kappa = 2k and K = x0 sqrt(k/D) = z/sqrt(2); gamma = k/D. The text after their Eq. (2) prints alpha = sqrt(r + s/D); the equation follows with alpha^2 = (r + s)/D, which is what is used here (the two agree at D = 1; checked against this entry at D = 0.5).

## When it holds

One dimension, harmonic trap with the target at its centre, start and reset point on one side, Poissonian instantaneous resetting. Exact. A target away from the centre is a different problem.
