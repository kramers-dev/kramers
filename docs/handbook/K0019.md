# K0019. Mean completion time under sharp (periodic) restart

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

If a process is restarted deterministically every tau, its mean completion time is the integral of its survival probability up to tau, divided by the probability of completing within one period.

## Model

$$ \text{any process with survival } S(t),\ \text{restart every } \tau $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_\tau\rangle=\frac{\int_0^{\tau}S(t)\,dt}{1-S(\tau)} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import sharp_restart_mean_time
sharp_restart_mean_time(survival: 'Callable[[float], float]', period: 'float') -> 'float'
```

```text
Mean completion time when the process restarts every ``period``: int_0^period S dt / (1 - S(period)).

``survival`` is S(t), the probability that the process without restart has not
completed by t.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `survival` | S(t) without restart |
| `period` | restart period tau |

## When it holds

Any process; instantaneous restart from scratch every tau. Exact.
