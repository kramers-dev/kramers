# K0009. Firing rate of the quadratic integrate-and-fire neuron with white noise

**Area:** Neuron models. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

The stationary firing rate of the quadratic integrate-and-fire (theta) neuron driven by white noise, for any mean input mu (below or above threshold), as one integral. It is the reciprocal of the passage time through a noisy saddle-node (K0004).

## Model

$$ \tau_m\dot v = v^2 + \mu + \tau_m^{1/2}\sigma\,\eta(t),\ \text{spike at } v=+\infty,\ \text{reset to } -\infty $$

## Result

$$ \nu = \frac{1}{\pi\tau_m}\left[\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{d\xi}{\pi^{1/2}}\exp(-\mu\xi^2-\sigma^4\xi^6/48)\right]^{-1} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import qif_firing_rate
qif_firing_rate(mu: 'float', sigma: 'float', tau_m: 'float') -> 'float'
```

```text
Stationary rate of tau_m dv/dt = v^2 + mu + tau_m^(1/2) sigma eta(t), spikes at +inf, reset to -inf.

Brunel and Latham's single-integral form, Eq. (3.18a): nu = 1 / (pi tau_m) / I with
I = integral over xi of exp(-mu xi^2 - sigma^4 xi^6 / 48) / sqrt(pi).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `mu` | mean input (dimensionless current) |
| `sigma` | noise amplitude |
| `tau_m` | membrane time constant |

## When it holds

White noise (synaptic time constant negligible against tau_m); any mu. Exact.
