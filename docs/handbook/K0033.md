# K0033. Run-and-tumble particle in d dimensions: universal survival probability

**Area:** Active and anomalous motion. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

For a run-and-tumble particle that starts at the origin in any dimension, tumbles at constant rate gamma into an isotropic new direction and runs at any speed distribution, the probability that its x-component has stayed positive up to t is exp(-gamma t/2)[I0(gamma t/2) + I1(gamma t/2)]/2: independent of d and of the speed, starting at 1/2 and decaying as 1/sqrt(pi gamma t).

## Model

$$ \dot{\mathbf{x}}=v\,\mathbf{n}(t),\ \mathbf{n}\ \text{redrawn isotropically at rate}\ \gamma,\ \mathbf{x}(0)=0;\ S(t)=\Pr[x_1(t')>0\ \forall t'\le t] $$

## Result

$$ S(t)=\frac{1}{2}e^{-\gamma t/2}\left[I_0\!\left(\frac{\gamma t}{2}\right)+I_1\!\left(\frac{\gamma t}{2}\right)\right] $$

## When it holds

Exponential run times (constant tumble rate), isotropic new directions, start at the origin with a random direction, no translational diffusion. Exact in every dimension and for any speed distribution.
