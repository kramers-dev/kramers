# K0026. Force-dependent escape rate of Dudko, Hummer and Szabo

**Area:** Driven escape and switching under a ramp. **Kind:** asymptotic (leading term of an expansion; its error is mapped against an exact reference).

Under a constant force F, the escape rate over a barrier of height dG at distance x is k0 (1 - nu F x/dG)^(1/nu - 1) exp{dG [1 - (1 - nu F x/dG)^(1/nu)]/kT}: nu = 2/3 for a linear-cubic surface, 1/2 for a harmonic well with a cusp, and nu = 1 gives Bell's exponential law. For nu = 2/3 it is exactly Kramers' formula for the tilted surface, the large-barrier limit of the noisy saddle-node.

## Model

$$ U(y)=U_0(y)-Fy,\ \text{overdamped},\ \nu=2/3:\ U_0=\tfrac32\Delta G^\ddagger y/x^\ddagger-2\Delta G^\ddagger(y/x^\ddagger)^3 $$

## Result

$$ k(F)=k_0\left(1-\frac{\nu Fx^\ddagger}{\Delta G^\ddagger}\right)^{1/\nu-1}\exp\left\{\beta\Delta G^\ddagger\left[1-\left(1-\frac{\nu Fx^\ddagger}{\Delta G^\ddagger}\right)^{1/\nu}\right]\right\} $$


```text
k(F) = k0 (1 - nu F x/dG)^(1/nu - 1) exp{dG/kT [1 - (1 - nu F x/dG)^(1/nu)]}.

nu = 2/3: linear-cubic free-energy surface; nu = 1/2: harmonic well with a cusp;
nu = 1: Bell's model (then x is force-independent). F < dG/(nu x) is required;
near it the barrier vanishes and the rate picture fails.
```

## When it holds

Force below the critical force dG/(nu x) with a barrier of several kT left; overdamped, single barrier, soft pulling device. For nu = 2/3 the error against the exact rate is that of Kramers' formula (K0001: 3% at a 10 kT barrier).
