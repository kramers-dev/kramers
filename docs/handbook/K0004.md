# K0004. Mean passage time through a noisy saddle-node, closed form in Airy functions

**Area:** Escape over a barrier. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

The mean time for dx/dt = x^2 + epsilon0 + g0 xi to go from minus to plus infinity is g0^(-2/3) 2^(1/3) pi^2 [Ai^2 + Bi^2] of -2^(2/3) epsilon0 / g0^(4/3), at any distance from the bifurcation and any barrier height, on either side of the fold.

## Model

$$ \dot x = x^2 + \epsilon_0 + g_0\,\xi(t),\quad x: -\infty \to +\infty $$

## Result

$$ \tau = g_0^{-2/3}\,\mathcal{T}(\alpha),\ \alpha=\epsilon_0/g_0^{4/3},\ \mathcal{T}(\alpha)=2^{1/3}\pi^2\left[\mathrm{Ai}^2(-2^{2/3}\alpha)+\mathrm{Bi}^2(-2^{2/3}\alpha)\right] $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import passage_time_saddle_node
passage_time_saddle_node(epsilon0: 'ArrayLike', g0: 'float') -> 'np.ndarray | float'
```

```text
Mean time from -inf to +inf for dx/dt = x^2 + epsilon0 + g0 xi(t).

tau = g0^(-2/3) T(alpha), alpha = epsilon0 / g0^(4/3), with Hathcock and Sethna's
T(alpha) = 2^(1/3) pi^2 [Ai^2(-2^(2/3) alpha) + Bi^2(-2^(2/3) alpha)].
epsilon0 < 0 is below the bifurcation (a well and a barrier of 4/3 |epsilon0|^(3/2)).
With noise written as sqrt(2D) xi, g0 = sqrt(2D).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `epsilon0` | distance from the bifurcation (negative: a barrier) |
| `g0` | noise amplitude (g0 = sqrt(2D)) |

## When it holds

Constant (additive) white noise; start at -infinity, absorption at +infinity. Exact.

## Notes

This is the mean passage time. Its reciprocal is the firing rate of the quadratic integrate-and-fire neuron (K0009) but not the quasi-stationary decay rate (K0008); the two differ by 43% at the bifurcation point.

**Also known as:** noisy saddle-node bifurcation; mean interspike interval of the theta neuron; quadratic integrate-and-fire neuron; barrier crossing at any barrier height; Airy function passage time.
