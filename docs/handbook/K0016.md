# K0016. Optimal resetting rate for diffusion to a target

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

The mean time for a resetting Brownian searcher to find a target at distance x0 is smallest at the rate r* = z*^2 D/x0^2, where z* = 1.59362... solves z/2 = 1 - exp(-z).

## Model

$$ \text{as K0015} $$

## Result

$$ r^*=\frac{(z^*)^2D}{x_0^2},\quad \frac{z^*}{2}=1-e^{-z^*},\ z^*=1.59362\ldots $$


