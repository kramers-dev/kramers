# K0021. Mean velocity in a tilted periodic potential (Stratonovich's formula)

**Area:** Transport in periodic potentials. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

An overdamped Brownian particle in a periodic potential tilted by a constant force F drifts with a long-time mean velocity given by one double integral over a period. It covers the whole range from the locked state to the running state, through the critical tilt where the barriers vanish.

## Model

$$ \eta\dot x=-V'(x)+F+\sqrt{2\eta k_BT}\,\xi(t),\quad V(x+L)=V(x) $$

## Result

$$ \langle\dot x\rangle=\frac{1-e^{-LF/k_BT}}{\int_{x_0}^{x_0+L}\frac{dx}{L}I_+(x)},\quad I_\pm(x)=\int_0^L\frac{dy}{D_0}e^{[\pm V(x)\mp V(x\mp y)-yF]/k_BT} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import tilted_periodic_velocity
tilted_periodic_velocity(V: 'Func', F: 'float', L: 'float', D0: 'float', kT: 'float' = 1.0) -> 'float'
```

```text
Long-time mean velocity for eta dx/dt = -V'(x) + F + noise, V of period L, D0 = kT/eta.

<v> = (1 - exp(-L F / kT)) / [(1/L) integral over one period of I+(x)] (Reimann et al. Eq. 7).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `V` | periodic potential (vectorised) |
| `F` | tilting force |
| `L` | period |
| `D0` | free diffusion coefficient kT/eta |
| `kT` | thermal energy |

## When it holds

One-dimensional overdamped motion, white noise, any periodic V and any F. Exact.

## How it is checked

| Check | Against | Latest result |
|---|---|---|
| no potential: F / eta | free drift | pass: worst 4.4e-16 (tolerance 1e-12), 2026-10-25 |
| against L over the mean time per period | quadrature solver, natural boundary behind the tilt | pass: worst 5.6e-16 (tolerance 1e-09), 2026-10-25 |

## Notes

Dagdug and Berezhkovskii (2009) restate I+- with the opposite overall sign in the exponent; the sign here is the PRL's, confirmed by the free-drift check.
