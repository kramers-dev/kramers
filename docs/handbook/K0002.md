# K0002. Exact mean first-passage time in one dimension (quadrature formula)

**Area:** Escape over a barrier. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

The mean time for an overdamped particle in a potential U to leave an interval is a double integral of exp(U/kT) and exp(-U/kT): the Pontryagin-Andronov-Vitt formula. It holds at any barrier height and for any potential shape, and it is the exact reference against which rate formulas such as Kramers' are judged.

## Model

$$ dx = -D\,U'(x)\,dt + \sqrt{2D}\,dW,\ a<x<b,\ U \text{ in } k_BT $$

## Result

$$ t(x) = \frac{1}{D}\int_x^b dy\, e^{U(y)}\int_a^y dz\, e^{-U(z)}\quad (a \text{ reflecting},\ b \text{ absorbing}) $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import mean_first_passage_time
mean_first_passage_time(potential: 'Callable[[np.ndarray], np.ndarray]', x0: 'ArrayLike', a: 'float', b: 'float', D: 'float' = 1.0, left: 'str' = 'reflecting', right: 'str' = 'absorbing') -> 'np.ndarray | float'
```

```text
Mean time to leave (a, b) from x0 for dx = -D U'(x) dt + sqrt(2D) dW, U in kT.

``left`` and ``right`` are "reflecting" or "absorbing" (at least one absorbing);
infinite ends are allowed when the potential confines ("natural") or the drift
expels the particle in finite time ("exit"). Reflecting at a and absorbing at b is
Haenggi, Talkner and Borkovec's Eq. (7.8); both absorbing is their Eq. (7.7) with
a misprinted sign corrected (see the entry notes).
```

## When it holds

One-dimensional overdamped (Fokker-Planck) dynamics with white noise; exact.

## Notes

Question for the authors (Haenggi, Talkner and Borkovec). In Eq. (7.7) as printed (journal scan, p. 291), the inner integral in the numerator of the first term has exp[U(z)/kBT]. With that sign the formula does not vanish at the absorbing end: for U = x^3/3 - x on [-2, 1] with kT = D = 1 it gives t(b) = 2.511 instead of 0. With exp[-U(z)/kBT] it vanishes there and agrees with this entry to 1e-10. A flat potential cannot reveal the difference. We read this as a misprint but may have misread the notation; no erratum was found in Crossref.
