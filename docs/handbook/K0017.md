# K0017. Any first-passage process under Poissonian restart: mean and second moment

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

If a process that completes after a random time T is restarted from scratch at rate r, the mean completion time is (1 - T~(r))/(r T~(r)), where T~ is the Laplace transform of the density of T. The second moment also needs only T~ and its slope.

## Model

$$ \text{any process with completion time } T,\ \tilde T(s)=\langle e^{-sT}\rangle,\ \text{restart at rate } r $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_r\rangle=\frac{1-\tilde T(r)}{r\,\tilde T(r)},\quad \langle T_r^2\rangle=\frac{2\left(r\,\tilde T'(r)-\tilde T(r)+1\right)}{r^2\tilde T(r)^2} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import restart_moments
restart_moments(laplace_fpt: 'Callable[[float], float]', r: 'float', h: 'float | None' = None) -> 'RestartMoments'
```

```text
Moments of the completion time under restart at rate r, from the Laplace transform T~(s) of the
first-passage-time density without restart (Reuveni's Eq. 7).

mean = (1 - T~(r)) / (r T~(r)); second = 2 (r T~'(r) - T~(r) + 1) / (r^2 T~(r)^2).
The derivative is taken by a central difference with step ``h`` (default 1e-4 r).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `laplace_fpt` | s -> T~(s) |
| `r` | restart rate |
| `h` | step for the derivative |

## When it holds

Any first-passage process; restarts at exponentially distributed times, instantaneous, from scratch. Exact.
