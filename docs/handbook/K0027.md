# K0027. Mean and variance of the rupture force under constant loading (Dudko, Hummer and Szabo)

**Area:** Driven escape and switching under a ramp. **Kind:** asymptotic (leading term of an expansion; its error is mapped against an exact reference).

For a bond with the rate of K0026 pulled at constant loading rate, the mean rupture force and its variance have closed forms in the logarithm of the loading rate, which reduce to the Bell-Evans logarithmic law for nu = 1.

## Model

$$ \text{K0026 rate},\ F(t)=\dot F\,t $$

## Result

$$ \langle F\rangle\cong\frac{\Delta G^\ddagger}{\nu x^\ddagger}\left\{1-\left[\frac{1}{\beta\Delta G^\ddagger}\ln\frac{k_0k_BTe^{\beta\Delta G^\ddagger+\gamma}}{x^\ddagger\dot F}\right]^{\nu}\right\},\ \sigma_F^2\cong\frac{\pi^2(k_BT)^2}{6x^{\ddagger2}}\left[\frac{1}{\beta\Delta G^\ddagger}\ln\frac{k_0k_BTe^{\beta\Delta G^\ddagger+\tilde\gamma}}{x^\ddagger\dot F}\right]^{2\nu-2} $$

```text
<F> and var F for constant loading rate (Dudko, Hummer and Szabo Eqs. 5-6, kT restored).

<F> = dG/(nu x) {1 - [kT/dG ln(k0 kT e^(dG/kT + gamma) / (x Fdot))]^nu}
var F = pi^2 kT^2 / (6 x^2) [kT/dG ln(k0 kT e^(dG/kT + gamma~) / (x Fdot))]^(2 nu - 2)
with gamma = 0.5772 and gamma~ = gamma^2 - (3/pi^2) psi''(1) = 1.0638.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `loading_rate` | dF/dt |
| `k0` | zero-force rate |
| `x` | x^ddagger |
| `dG` | barrier |
| `nu` | 2/3, 1/2 or 1 |
| `kT` | thermal energy |

## When it holds

Several kT of barrier at the typical rupture force; quasi-static (adiabatic) loading. Against the exact moments of the same model (computed check): within 2% at dG = 20 kT, better at higher barriers.
