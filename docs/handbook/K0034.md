# K0034. Drift-diffusion to a target under restart: mean, spread and the Peclet transition

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A particle that diffuses with drift V towards a target at distance L, and is returned to its start at rate r, reaches the target after a mean time (e^z - 1)/r with z = L(sqrt(V^2 + 4Dr) - V)/(2D). Restart can shorten the mean only when the Peclet number LV/(2D) is below 1; above it the best rate is zero.

## Model

$$ \partial_t p=-V\partial_x p+D\partial_x^2 p-r\,p+r\,\delta(x)\,Q(t),\ x\in(-\infty,L),\ p(L,t)=0,\ x(0)=0 $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_r\rangle=\frac{e^{z}-1}{r},\quad \sigma(T_r)=\frac{1}{r}\sqrt{e^{2z}-2e^{z}\frac{Lr}{\sqrt{V^2+4Dr}}-1},\quad z=\frac{L}{2D}\left(\sqrt{V^2+4Dr}-V\right) $$

```text
Mean and standard deviation of the time to reach a target at distance L, with drift V towards it,
restarting from the start at rate r.

mean = (e^z - 1)/r and std = sqrt(e^(2z) - 2 e^z L r / sqrt(V^2 + 4 D r) - 1)/r, with
z = L (sqrt(V^2 + 4 D r) - V) / (2 D). <x^2> = 2 D t without drift; V < 0 is drift away
from the target. The standard deviation is evaluated in a rearranged form that keeps its
digits as r -> 0 (where it tends to the spread of the passage time without restart).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `L` | distance from the start (and restart point) to the target |
| `V` | drift towards the target (negative: away) |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient (<x^2> = 2Dt) |
| `r` | restart rate |

## When it holds

One dimension, constant drift and diffusion, an absorbing target and no other boundary, Poissonian instantaneous restart to the start. Exact. V < 0 (drift away) is allowed for r > 0.
