# K0028. Force-dependent lifetimes from rupture-force histograms (Dudko, Hummer and Szabo, 2008)

**Area:** Driven escape and switching under a ramp. **Kind:** estimator (turns measured data into a quantity of the model).

A histogram of rupture forces at one loading rate gives the lifetime of the bond at each force: the probability of surviving to that force divided by the density of ruptures there and the loading rate. Histograms from different loading rates must collapse onto one curve if the rate picture holds, which is a test of the model.

## Model

$$ \tau(F)=\frac{\int_F^\infty p(f)\,df}{\dot F(F)\,p(F)} $$

## Result

$$ \tau\big(F_0+(k-\tfrac12)\Delta F\big)=\frac{\left(h_k/2+\sum_{i=k+1}^{N}h_i\right)\Delta F}{h_k\,\dot F\big(F_0+(k-\tfrac12)\Delta F\big)} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import histogram_to_lifetime
histogram_to_lifetime(counts: 'ArrayLike', edges: 'ArrayLike', loading_rate: 'float | Callable[[np.ndarray], np.ndarray]') -> 'LifetimeEstimate'
```

```text
tau(F) at bin centres: (h_k/2 + sum_{i>k} h_i) dF / (h_k Fdot(F_k)) (Eq. [10] of DHS 2008).

``loading_rate`` may depend on force (a linker). ``stderr`` is a delta-method error
from multinomial counts, a computed addition. Empty bins, and bins with no ruptures
above them, give NaN.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `counts` | ruptures per force bin |
| `edges` | bin edges |
| `loading_rate` | dF/dt, number or function of F |

## When it holds

Rate process with single-exponential kinetics at constant force; quasi-adiabatic loading; irreversible rupture; every rupture inside the histogram. On an exact histogram the estimator is too high by a factor lambda / (2 tanh(lambda/2)), about 1 + lambda^2/12, where lambda = ln(S_k/S_k+1) (computed check): 0.09% when 10% of the survivors fail in a bin, 0.7% at 25%, 4% at 50%. The logarithmic form (K0025) has no such bias.
