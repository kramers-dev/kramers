# K0035. Resetting in a logarithmic potential: mean and spread of the time to the origin

**Area:** Stochastic resetting. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A particle diffusing in the potential U0 ln|x| and reset to x0 at rate r reaches the origin after a mean time [2^(nu-1) Gamma(nu)/(y^nu K_nu(y)) - 1]/r, with nu = (1 + beta U0)/2 and y = sqrt(r/D) x0. Resetting shortens the mean only for beta U0 < 5, where the spread of the time without resetting exceeds its mean.

## Model

$$ \partial_t p=\partial_x\!\left[\frac{U_0}{\zeta x}p\right]+D\,\partial_x^2p-r\,p+r\,\delta(x-x_0)\,Q(t),\ p(0,t)=0,\ D=k_BT/\zeta $$

## Result

$$ \langle T_r\rangle=\frac{1}{r}\left[\frac{2^{\nu-1}\Gamma(\nu)}{(\alpha_0x_0)^{\nu}K_\nu(\alpha_0x_0)}-1\right],\quad \nu=\frac{1+\beta U_0}{2},\ \alpha_0=\sqrt{r/D} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import log_potential_restart
log_potential_restart(x0: 'float', beta_U0: 'float', D: 'float', r: 'float') -> 'RestartTime'
```

```text
Mean and standard deviation of the time for a particle in U(x) = U0 ln|x| to reach the origin from x0 > 0,
resetting to x0 at rate r.

With nu = (1 + beta U0)/2 and y = sqrt(r/D) x0 (Eqs. 29 and 31):
mean = [2^(nu-1) Gamma(nu) / (y^nu K_nu(y)) - 1]/r,
std = sqrt(2^(nu-1) Gamma(nu) [2^(nu-1) Gamma(nu) - y^(1+nu) K_(nu-1)(y)] / (y^(2 nu) K_nu(y)^2) - 1)/r.
beta U0 > -1 is required (otherwise the particle is pushed away and need not reach the origin);
beta U0 = 0 is free diffusion (entry K0015). <x^2> = 2 D t for U0 = 0.

For y >= 0.5 the printed Bessel forms are evaluated in logarithms. For y < 0.5, where they lose
digits (the bracket tends to 1), the same moments are computed from the renewal formula with the
transform written as an expectation over the Gamma-distributed variable of Eq. (5): 1 - T~(r) =
E[1 - e^-w] and r T~'(r) - T~(r) + 1 = E[1 - e^-w (1 + w)], w = y^2/(4G), which has no cancellation.
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `x0` | start and reset position (> 0) |
| `beta_U0` | strength of the potential U0 ln|x| in units of kT (> -1) |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient (<x^2> = 2Dt for U0 = 0) |
| `r` | resetting rate |

## When it holds

One dimension, the half-line x > 0 with the origin absorbing, potential U0 ln|x| with beta U0 > -1, Poissonian instantaneous resetting to the start. Exact. Without resetting the mean is finite only for beta U0 > 1 and the spread only for beta U0 > 3.
