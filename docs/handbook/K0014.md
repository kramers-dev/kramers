# K0014. Exit time of Brownian motion from a ball in d dimensions: mean and second moment

**Area:** Diffusion to a target. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

A Brownian particle with diffusion coefficient D started at distance r from the centre of an absorbing sphere of radius R in d dimensions leaves after a mean time (R^2 - r^2)/(2dD); the second moment is also in closed form.

## Model

$$ \partial_t p = D\nabla^2 p\ \text{in}\ |x|<R\subset\mathbb{R}^d,\ p=0\ \text{on}\ |x|=R $$

## Result

$$ \langle\tau\rangle=\frac{R^2-r^2}{2dD},\quad \langle\tau^2\rangle=\frac{(R^2-r^2)\left[(d+4)R^2-dr^2\right]}{4d^2(d+2)D^2} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import ball_exit_time
ball_exit_time(r: 'float', R: 'float', D: 'float', d: 'int') -> 'BallExit'
```

```text
Exit time of free diffusion (coefficient D) from the ball of radius R in d dimensions, from radius r.

Chen and Huang's Eqs. (16)-(18): mean (R^2 - r^2) / (2 d D), second moment
(R^2 - r^2) [(d + 4) R^2 - d r^2] / (4 d^2 (d + 2) D^2).
```

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `r` | start radius |
| `R` | ball radius |
| `D` | diffusion coefficient |
| `d` | dimension |

## Where it is printed

- **Primary source:** H. Chen and F. Huang, Phys. Rev. E 105, 034109 (2022). [doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.105.034109](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.105.034109)
  - Where: Eqs. (16)-(18) (arXiv:2109.11101v2 numbering).
  - Read: arXiv:2109.11101v2 HTML (posted after journal publication), opened 14 Oct 2026.
  - As printed: $ \langle\bar{\tau}_0(\bar r)\rangle=\frac{1-\bar r^{2}}{2d};\ \langle\bar\tau_0^2(\bar r)\rangle=\frac{(1-\bar r^2)[(d+4)-d\bar r^2]}{4d^2(d+2)};\ \bar r=r/R,\ \bar\tau=\tau D/R^{2} $
  - Notation: no resetting (gamma = 0).

## When it holds

Free diffusion, absorbing sphere, no resetting. Exact.
