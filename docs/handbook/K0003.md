# K0003. Splitting probability between two absorbing ends in one dimension

**Area:** Diffusion to a target. **Kind:** exact (exact within the stated model).

For a diffusion in a potential U(x) with a position-dependent diffusion coefficient D(x), started at x0 between two absorbing points a < x0 < b, the probability of reaching b first is the integral of exp(U)/D from a to x0 divided by the same integral from a to b.

## Model

$$ dX = [-D(X)\,U'(X) + D'(X)]\,dt + \sqrt{2D(X)}\,dW,\quad a < X < b,\ U \text{ in } k_BT $$

## Result

$$ \pi_b(x_0) = \frac{\int_a^{x_0} e^{U(z)}\,dz/D(z)}{\int_a^{b} e^{U(z)}\,dz/D(z)} $$

## Use it

```python
from kramers.formulas import splitting_probability
splitting_probability(potential: 'Callable[[np.ndarray], np.ndarray]', x0: 'ArrayLike', a: 'float', b: 'float', D: 'float | Callable[[np.ndarray], np.ndarray]' = 1.0) -> 'np.ndarray | float'
```

```text
Probability of reaching ``b`` before ``a`` from ``x0``.

Model: dX = [-D U'(X) + D'(X)] dt + sqrt(2 D(X)) dW (Ito), U in units of kT, so that
exp(-U) is the equilibrium density whatever D(x) is.

x0

a, b
    The two absorbing ends.
D
    Diffusion coefficient, a number or a function of x.
```

## Where it is printed

- **Primary source:** L. Dagdug and A. M. Berezhkovskii, J. Chem. Phys. 131, 056101 (2009). [doi:10.1063/1.3179679](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3179679)
  - Where: Eq. (6).
  - Read: PubMed Central PMC2736570 (MathML), opened 14 Oct 2026.
  - As printed: $ W_R(x_0)=\frac{\int_{x_L}^{x_0}e^{\beta U(z)}\frac{dz}{D(z)}}{\int_{x_L}^{x_R}e^{\beta U(z)}\frac{dz}{D(z)}} $
  - Notation: x_L = a, x_R = b, beta U = our U (in kT), W_R = pi_b.
- **Also printed in:** S. Redner, Physica A 631, 128545 (2023). [doi:10.1016/j.physa.2023.128545](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2023.128545)
  - Where: Eq. (4.12), p. 6 (constant drift).
  - Read: journal PDF on the author's site, opened 14 Oct 2026.
  - As printed: $ \mathcal{E}_{+}(x_0)=\frac{1-e^{-vx_0/D}}{1-e^{-vL/D}} $
  - Notation: interval [0, L]; constant drift v = D F, i.e. U = -F x.

## When it holds

Markovian one-dimensional diffusion; both ends absorbing; D(x) > 0. Exact.
