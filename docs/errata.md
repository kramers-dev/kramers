## K0002. Exact mean first-passage time in one dimension (quadrature formula)

Question for the authors (Haenggi, Talkner and Borkovec). In Eq. (7.7) as printed (journal scan, p. 291), the inner integral in the numerator of the first term has exp[U(z)/kBT]. With that sign the formula does not vanish at the absorbing end: for U = x^3/3 - x on [-2, 1] with kT = D = 1 it gives t(b) = 2.511 instead of 0. With exp[-U(z)/kBT] it vanishes there and agrees with this entry to 1e-10. A flat potential cannot reveal the difference. We read this as a misprint but may have misread the notation; no erratum was found in Crossref.
