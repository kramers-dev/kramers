# Questions for authors

Printed equations that disagree with our computations. Each case was found when an entry was implemented and checked, and each is stated with the computation, so that it can be rerun. We think these are misprints, but we list them as questions until the authors have answered: we may have misread the notation. We have not checked whether any of them entered computations in the papers, and we found no published erratum for them. If you are an author, please tell us in an issue; we will correct this page and credit you.

## K0002. Exact mean first-passage time in one dimension (quadrature formula)

Question for the authors (Haenggi, Talkner and Borkovec). In Eq. (7.7) as printed (journal scan, p. 291), the inner integral in the numerator of the first term has exp[U(z)/kBT]. With that sign the formula does not vanish at the absorbing end: for U = x^3/3 - x on [-2, 1] with kT = D = 1 it gives t(b) = 2.511 instead of 0. With exp[-U(z)/kBT] it vanishes there and agrees with this entry to 1e-10. A flat potential cannot reveal the difference. We read this as a misprint but may have misread the notation: on the author-hosted scan we read, a speck sits on the sign, so the printed sign must be confirmed on a clean copy before the authors are asked. No erratum or comment was found in Crossref, PubMed or an OpenAlex search of citing works (15 Oct 2026).

## K0011. Variance of the passage time through a noisy saddle-node, any bias and thresholds

Questions for the authors (Lindner, Longtin and Bulsara), both from computations. (1) Eq. (3.2) as printed has the prefactor (9/D)^(2/3); with it, Eq. (3.2) gives exactly half of the paper's own Eq. (3.5) at beta = 0 (computed ratio 2.0000 at beta = 0, -0.5 and 0.8), and with 2 (9/D)^(2/3) the two agree. (2) In Eq. (A.3) as printed the middle integrand is exp(-V(z)/D); for a linear potential V = -mu x that gives 8.3e3 instead of the exact 2 D L / mu^3 = 0.728 (mu = 1.3, D = 0.4, L = 2), while exp(+V(z)/D) reproduces it. Eqs. (A.1), (A.2) and (A.4) are consistent with each other and with this entry. We read both as misprints but may have misread the notation. The bare prefactor of Eq. (3.2) reappears in Vilela and Lindner, J. Theor. Biol. 257, 90 (2009), Eq. (38) of the arXiv version (journal version not checked).
