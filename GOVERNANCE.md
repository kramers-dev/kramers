# Governance

Roles follow work. No title is given before the work exists.

| Role | How you get it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor | One merged entry, check or fix | Named on the release |
| Checker | Confirmed an entry, usually one from your own paper | Named on that entry |
| Area editor | Sustained review of entries in one of the areas | Reviews and merges in that area; named in `.github/CODEOWNERS` |
| Maintainer | Agreement of the existing maintainers | Merges anywhere; makes releases |

The founding maintainer is Alexander Sokol. A second maintainer from another
institution is the first priority of the project.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day: the reviewer of a pull request decides, using CONTRIBUTING.md.
- Scope, the entry rules, releases: the maintainers, by consensus, in a public issue
  open for at least a week.
- When there are five maintainers from at least three institutions, a steering group
  of three is elected by the maintainers each year.

## Disputes about an entry

An entry whose correctness is disputed is marked "disputed" on its page, with the
argument, until the dispute is settled by a check that both sides accept. Corrections
are made in public and credited.

## Changes to this file

By the maintainers, in a public issue open for at least a week.
